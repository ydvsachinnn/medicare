# Diet & Nutrition Recommendations Guide (WHO/NIH)

## Diet for Diabetes

### Goals
- Maintain stable blood sugar levels
- Achieve and maintain healthy weight
- Prevent complications

### Recommended Foods
- **Whole Grains:** Brown rice, oats, quinoa, whole wheat bread, barley
- **Vegetables:** Spinach, broccoli, cauliflower, cucumber, bitter gourd (karela), beans, capsicum
- **Fruits (Low GI):** Apples, berries, pears, oranges, guava (in moderation)
- **Lean Proteins:** Grilled chicken, fish, eggs, tofu, lentils, dal
- **Healthy Fats:** Olive oil, nuts (almonds, walnuts), seeds (flaxseeds, chia seeds)
- **Legumes:** Chickpeas, kidney beans, black beans

### Foods to Avoid
- White rice, white bread, maida (refined flour)
- Sugary drinks: sodas, fruit juices, sweetened tea/coffee
- Sweets: cakes, pastries, candy, ice cream
- Fried foods and trans fats
- Processed snacks and packaged foods
- Excessive fruit intake (especially high GI: watermelon, pineapple, mango)

### Meal Tips
- Eat 5-6 small meals instead of 3 large meals
- Do not skip breakfast
- Include protein and fiber in every meal
- Monitor portion sizes
- Stay hydrated with water and herbal teas

## Diet for Hypertension (DASH Diet)

### Goals
- Reduce blood pressure naturally
- Reduce sodium intake
- Increase potassium, calcium, and magnesium

### Recommended Foods
- **Fruits:** Bananas, oranges, berries, melons, apples
- **Vegetables:** Leafy greens (spinach, kale), broccoli, carrots, tomatoes, sweet potatoes
- **Whole Grains:** Oats, brown rice, whole wheat
- **Low-Fat Dairy:** Milk, yogurt, cheese (low sodium)
- **Lean Proteins:** Fish (salmon, tuna), skinless chicken, beans, lentils
- **Nuts and Seeds:** Almonds, walnuts, sunflower seeds, flaxseeds
- **Healthy Oils:** Olive oil, canola oil

### Foods to Avoid
- Salt: limit to less than 2,300 mg/day (ideally 1,500 mg/day)
- Processed and packaged foods (high sodium)
- Pickles, canned soups, soy sauce, ketchup
- Red meat and processed meats (sausages, bacon, salami)
- Sugary drinks and sweets
- Excessive caffeine
- Alcohol

## Diet for Fever

### Goals
- Stay hydrated
- Provide easy-to-digest nutrition
- Support immune system

### Recommended Foods & Fluids
- **Fluids:** Water, coconut water, herbal teas, warm soups, ORS
- **Soups:** Chicken broth, vegetable soup, tomato soup
- **Soft Foods:** Khichdi, porridge (daliya), rice with dal, bananas
- **Fruits:** Oranges, watermelon, grapes, papaya
- **Protein:** Boiled eggs, steamed chicken, paneer
- **Warm Drinks:** Ginger tea, turmeric milk (haldi doodh), lemon water with honey

### Foods to Avoid
- Fried and oily foods
- Spicy foods
- Heavy, hard-to-digest meals
- Cold drinks and ice cream
- Processed and junk food

---

## Diet for Kidney Disease

### Goals
- Reduce kidney workload
- Control waste buildup in blood
- Manage fluid and electrolyte balance

### General Guidelines
- **Low Sodium:** Less than 2,000 mg/day
- **Low Potassium:** Avoid bananas, oranges, potatoes, tomatoes, spinach
- **Low Phosphorus:** Limit dairy, nuts, seeds, dark sodas, processed meats
- **Moderate Protein:** As directed by nephrologist (too much protein creates waste)
- **Fluid Management:** Based on urine output and dialysis status

### Recommended Foods
- White rice, white bread, pasta (lower phosphorus than whole grains)
- Cabbage, bell peppers, onions, garlic, radish
- Apples, berries, grapes, pineapple (lower potassium fruits)
- Egg whites (high quality protein, low phosphorus)
- Fish, chicken (in moderation)

## Diet for Liver Disease

### Goals
- Support liver healing
- Reduce toxin load
- Manage fluid retention

### Recommended Foods
- **Complex Carbs:** Oats, brown rice, whole wheat
- **Lean Proteins:** Chicken, fish, tofu, eggs (moderate amounts)
- **Fruits & Vegetables:** Beets, berries, grapes, citrus fruits, leafy greens
- **Healthy Fats:** Olive oil, avocado (in moderation)
- **Hydration:** Adequate water intake

### Foods to STRICTLY Avoid
- **Alcohol:** Complete avoidance (essential)
- Fried and fatty foods
- Red meat and processed meats
- Raw or undercooked shellfish
- Excessive salt (for fluid retention management)
- Sugary foods and refined carbs

## Diet for Heart Disease

### Goals
- Reduce cholesterol and triglycerides
- Maintain healthy blood pressure
- Maintain healthy weight

### Recommended Foods (Mediterranean Diet)
- **Fish:** Salmon, mackerel, sardines (rich in omega-3)
- **Fruits & Vegetables:** Berries, citrus, leafy greens, tomatoes, broccoli
- **Whole Grains:** Oats, quinoa, brown rice, whole wheat
- **Healthy Fats:** Olive oil, avocado, nuts (almonds, walnuts)
- **Legumes:** Beans, lentils, chickpeas
- **Fiber:** Oats, beans, flaxseeds, psyllium husk

### Foods to Avoid
- Saturated fats: butter, ghee in excess, red meat, full-fat dairy
- Trans fats: margarine, fried foods, bakery items
- Processed meats: sausages, bacon, hot dogs
- Excessive sodium
- Sugary drinks and sweets
- Refined carbohydrates

## Diet During Pregnancy

### Nutritional Needs
- **Folic Acid (400-800 mcg/day):** Green leafy vegetables, fortified cereals, lentils, citrus fruits. Critical in first trimester for neural tube development.
- **Iron (27 mg/day):** Red meat, spinach, beans, fortified cereals, dried fruits
- **Calcium (1,000 mg/day):** Milk, yogurt, cheese, almonds, sesame seeds, ragi
- **Protein:** Eggs, chicken, fish, dal, paneer, tofu
- **Omega-3:** Salmon, walnuts, flaxseeds (avoid high-mercury fish: shark, swordfish, king mackerel)
- **Fiber:** Fruits, vegetables, whole grains (prevents constipation)
- **Water:** At least 8-10 glasses per day

### Foods to Avoid During Pregnancy
- Raw or undercooked meat, eggs, fish
- Unpasteurized dairy products
- High-mercury fish
- Excessive caffeine (limit to 200mg/day — about 1 cup of coffee)
- Alcohol (strict avoidance)
- Raw sprouts
- Processed and junk food
- Excess salt

## Diet for Weight Loss

### Goals
- Create healthy caloric deficit
- Ensure balanced nutrition
- Sustainable long-term approach

### Recommended Approach
- **Protein-Rich:** Eggs, chicken breast, fish, Greek yogurt, lentils, paneer (keeps you full longer)
- **High Fiber:** Oats, vegetables, fruits, whole grains, seeds
- **Healthy Fats:** Almonds, walnuts, olive oil, avocado
- **Complex Carbs:** Sweet potatoes, brown rice, quinoa
- **Hydration:** 8-10 glasses of water daily. Drink water before meals.

### Foods to Limit/Avoid
- Sugary drinks and fruit juices
- Fried foods and fast food
- White bread, white rice, maida products
- Sugary snacks, chocolates, desserts
- Excessive alcohol
- Processed and packaged foods

### Tips
- Eat slowly and mindfully
- Use smaller plates for portion control
- Don't skip meals (especially breakfast)
- Include protein in every meal
- Aim for 150-300 minutes of moderate exercise per week

## Diet for Weight Gain (Healthy)

### Goals
- Caloric surplus with nutritious foods
- Build muscle mass, not just fat
- Consistent eating schedule

### Recommended Foods
- **Calorie-Dense Nutritious Foods:** Nuts, dried fruits, peanut butter, cheese, whole milk
- **Protein-Rich:** Eggs, chicken, fish, paneer, dal, beans, protein shakes
- **Complex Carbs:** Rice, potatoes, sweet potatoes, whole wheat bread, oats
- **Healthy Fats:** Ghee, olive oil, coconut, avocado
- **Smoothies:** Banana + milk + oats + peanut butter + honey
- **Snacking:** Trail mix, granola bars, cheese sandwiches

### Tips
- Eat 5-6 meals per day
- Add extra calories to meals (cheese, nuts, ghee, oil)
- Drink calories: smoothies, shakes, milk
- Strength training to build muscle
- Don't fill up on water before meals

## Healthy Eating (General Guidelines)

### Balanced Plate
- Half the plate: fruits and vegetables
- Quarter of the plate: whole grains
- Quarter of the plate: lean protein
- Small amount of healthy fats
- Water as primary beverage

### Key Principles
- Eat a variety of colorful fruits and vegetables daily
- Choose whole grains over refined grains
- Include lean proteins from varied sources
- Limit added sugars to less than 10% of daily calories
- Limit sodium to less than 2,300 mg/day
- Choose healthy fats (unsaturated) over unhealthy fats (saturated, trans)
- Stay hydrated: 8-10 glasses of water daily
- Eat mindfully: chew slowly, enjoy your food
- Moderate portion sizes
- Cook at home more often

## Vaccination & Immunization Quick Reference

### Routine Adult Vaccinations
- **Influenza (Flu):** Annually, especially for high-risk groups
- **Tdap/Td (Tetanus, Diphtheria, Pertussis):** Booster every 10 years
- **Hepatitis B:** 3-dose series if not previously vaccinated
- **HPV (Human Papillomavirus):** Up to age 26 (can be given up to 45 in some guidelines)
- **Pneumococcal:** For adults 65+ or with chronic conditions
- **Herpes Zoster (Shingles):** For adults 50+
- **COVID-19:** Follow current WHO/local health authority recommendations

### Travel Vaccinations
- Hepatitis A
- Typhoid
- Yellow fever
- Japanese encephalitis
- Rabies (for high-risk travel)
- Consult travel medicine clinic 4-6 weeks before travel

## Medical Disclaimer
This information is for educational purposes only. Individual dietary needs vary based on age, sex, health conditions, and activity level. Always consult a registered dietitian or healthcare provider for personalized nutritional guidance.
