# Insomnia and Sleep Hygiene Clinical Guidelines (NIH/WHO)

## Insomnia Overview
Insomnia is a common sleep disorder characterized by difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or waking up too early and being unable to get back to sleep.
- Symptoms: Daytime fatigue, irritability, difficulty concentrating, anxiety about sleep, low energy.
- Common Causes: High stress, poor sleep hygiene, blue light exposure before bed, caffeine or alcohol consumption, shift work, anxiety, or underlying medical conditions.

## Proven Sleep Hygiene & Natural Remedies
1. Consistent Sleep Schedule: Go to bed and wake up at the exact same time every day, even on weekends.
2. Bedroom Environment: Keep your bedroom dark, quiet, and cool (65-68°F / 18-20°C).
3. Limit Stimulants: Avoid caffeine, tea, nicotine, and heavy meals after 4:00 PM. Avoid alcohol before bed.
4. Digital Screen Detox: Turn off smartphones, tablets, and laptops at least 1 hour prior to sleep.
5. Stimulus Control Therapy: Use your bed only for sleep. If unable to fall asleep after 20 minutes, get out of bed, go to a quiet room, read a book under low light, and return only when sleepy.
6. Relaxation Techniques: Try deep abdominal breathing (4-7-8 method), progressive muscle relaxation, or warm baths.

## Clinical Warnings
Seek medical evaluation from a physician or sleep specialist if insomnia lasts longer than 3 weeks or significantly impacts daily functioning.
