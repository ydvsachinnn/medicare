# MediCare Plus Hospital — FAQs, Services, & Department Guide

## Hospital Overview
MediCare Plus is a multi-specialty hospital providing comprehensive healthcare services including outpatient consultations, inpatient care, emergency services, diagnostic imaging, laboratory services, pharmacy, and surgical facilities.

## How to Book an Appointment

### Online Booking (Through Patient Portal)
1. Log in to your MediCare Plus patient account
2. Click "Book Appointment" in the dashboard sidebar
3. Select the department and preferred doctor
4. Choose your preferred date and available time slot
5. Add reason for visit (optional)
6. Submit the booking request
7. You will receive a confirmation once the appointment is approved

### Walk-In Registration
- Visit the reception desk with a valid government ID
- New patients will receive a unique patient ID
- Walk-in consultations are subject to doctor availability

### Emergency Consultation
- For urgent medical needs, select "Emergency" when booking
- Emergency appointments receive priority processing
- Direct walk-in to Emergency Department for critical conditions

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q: How do I access my medical reports?
Go to the "My Reports" section in your patient dashboard. All lab results and diagnostic reports uploaded by your doctor will be available there for viewing and download.

### Q: How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your registered email address. You will receive an OTP (One-Time Password) to reset your password.

### Q: How do I track my medication schedule?
Check the "Medication Timing Notifications" section on your dashboard. It shows your prescribed medicines with dosage, timing (morning/afternoon/evening/night), and food relationship (before/after food).

### Q: How do I view my prescriptions?
Go to "My Prescriptions" in the sidebar. You can view all active and past prescriptions, including medicine names, dosages, duration, and doctor's instructions.

### Q: Can I cancel or reschedule an appointment?
Contact the reception desk or use the appointment management section in your dashboard. Cancellations should be made at least 24 hours in advance.

### Q: What documents do I need for admission?
- Valid government ID (Aadhar, PAN, Passport, Driving License)
- Insurance card (if applicable)
- Previous medical records and reports
- Current medication list

### Q: Does the hospital accept health insurance?
Please contact the billing department for information about insurance partnerships and cashless facility availability.

### Q: What are the payment options?
- Cash
- Credit/Debit cards
- UPI payments
- Online bank transfer
- Insurance (cashless and reimbursement)

### Q: How can I get a copy of my discharge summary?
Discharge summaries are available in the "My Reports" section of your patient dashboard. You can also request a physical copy from the Medical Records department.

### Q: Is there a pharmacy inside the hospital?
Yes, MediCare Plus has an in-house pharmacy open 24/7 for emergency prescriptions and 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM for regular prescriptions.
